# Fictional demo data

20 tickets, 20 orders, 18 FAQ answers, 15 evaluation questions. The data uses example.com addresses and contains no real customer records.

Ticket mix: T01/T06-T10 are the six refund tickets; T02/T11-T14 are five delivery tickets; T03/T15-T18 are five product tickets; T04 is the angry customer; T05 and T19 are the Hinglish examples; T20 is off-topic. These labels intentionally overlap: the brief counts 20 examples by presentation role, not 20 mutually exclusive routing labels.

Dates from the original brief have been retained. Do not infer delivery time from order_date. Later refund tools need explicit fixture evidence for delivery time, report time, unopened/damaged state and a photo; those facts must not be invented.

Policies preserve the brief's numeric thresholds. FAQ details beyond the brief are fictional workshop content. The two tricky evaluation cases are late damage reporting and the refund approval threshold; they are not guaranteed to fail in a correct implementation.
