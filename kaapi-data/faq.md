# Kaapi & Co. demo FAQ

**Q: Do you deliver to Pune?**
A: Yes, we deliver across India. Pune orders usually arrive in 2-4 days.

**Q: Which grind should I use for a French press?**
A: Use a coarse grind.

**Q: Which grind should I use for cold brew?**
A: Use a coarse grind.

**Q: Which grind should I use for espresso?**
A: Use a fine grind suitable for your espresso machine.

**Q: Which grind should I use for pour-over?**
A: Use a medium grind as a starting point.

**Q: How much coffee should I use in a French press?**
A: Start with 15 grams of coffee for 250 millilitres of water and adjust to taste.

**Q: How long should French press coffee steep?**
A: Start with about 4 minutes, then adjust to taste.

**Q: Do you sell whole beans?**
A: Yes, choose whole beans when selecting your coffee.

**Q: Can I pause a subscription?**
A: Yes, contact support before your next dispatch to request a pause.

**Q: Can I change my subscription grind?**
A: Yes, contact support before the next dispatch.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription?**
A: Yes, contact support before the next dispatch to cancel future deliveries.

**Q: Do you deliver outside India?**
A: No, this demo brand delivers within India only.

**Q: Which payment methods are supported?**
A: UPI, debit cards, credit cards and net banking are supported.

**Q: Is cash on delivery available?**
A: COD is available for eligible Indian pincodes; check availability at checkout.

**Q: How should I store coffee?**
A: Keep coffee sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

**Q: Where can I find my order ID?**
A: Find it in your order confirmation email.

**Q: Can support promise an exact delivery date?**
A: Support can check the order status; an exact date is not guaranteed.

**Q: How do I report a damaged delivery?**
A: Contact support within 48 hours of delivery and include a photo and order ID.
