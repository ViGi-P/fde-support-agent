# Kaapi & Co. demo policies

## Returns
Unopened items can be returned within 7 days. Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with a photo. An opened bag is returnable only if it arrived damaged and is reported within 48 hours with a photo.

## Shipping
Metros: 2-4 days. Other cities: 4-7 days.

## Refunds
Refunds go to the original payment method within 5-7 working days.

## Approval rule
Any refund above ₹5,000 needs human approval.
